About this tool

This tool is educational. It describes patterns that can show up in organizations at different stages of TIC implementation not prescriptions, and not compliance guidance. It is designed to support organizational reflection and to connect what happens in real moments to TIO's Implementation Tool, Five Essential Elements, and Workforce Wellness framework.

Scenarios submitted through this tool may be considered for future versions. No identifying information is collected.
